California requires a contractor's licence for any electrical job over $500 in labour and materials. Unlicensed electrical work is the kind that fails an inspection, voids a home insurance claim, or quietly sits in a wall until something burns.

  • Active C-10 licence — the classification specific to electrical work.
  • $25,000 contractor's bond with American Contractors Indemnity, on file with the state.
  • Owner-operated — the person who holds the licence is the person doing the work.
  • Work to current code, permitted and inspected where the job requires it.
